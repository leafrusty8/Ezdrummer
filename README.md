## ⚡ Key Features
<a id="key-features"></a>

### Core Functionality:
- **Intuitive Workflow** — Quickly build realistic drum parts with drag-and-drop arrangements and easy MIDI groove access.
- **Tap2Find Technology** — Match your tapped rhythms to grooves for seamless integration into your projects.
- **Song Creator** — Effortlessly assemble full sections by combining related grooves, streamlining the transition from idea to structured track.
- **Flexible Compatibility** — Works as a standalone app or as a plug-in in hosts that support VST3 or AAX formats.
- **Integrated Mixer** — Adjust your drum tone using mix-ready presets and practical controls without switching between multiple windows.

### Advanced Features:
- **Grid Editor & Play Style Editing** — Gain hands-on control for fine-tuning timing, velocity, and note placement.
- **Detailed Articulations** — Experience expressiveness in performances with sampled kit pieces, ensuring a natural sound.
- **Storage Management** — Designed to handle large projects efficiently, though it requires meaningful disk space for the library expansions.
